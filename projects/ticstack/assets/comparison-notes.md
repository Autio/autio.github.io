# GTA VI overlay — assumptions

The original Tic-Tac-Toe and TicStack ratings are unchanged. All ratings are subjective design judgments, not measurements. GTA VI is explicitly a speculative projection informed by the GTA series, not a review of the unreleased game or scores supplied by Rockstar.

As checked on November 17, 2026, Rockstar lists November 19, 2026 as the release date: https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026

Projected GTA VI scores: planning depth 5, tactical variety 8, comeback potential 6, replay value 9, ease of learning 6, speed of play 2. These are illustrative hypotheses about action-sandbox play. The source verifies release status only and does not support these numerical judgments.

Planning concerns deliberate foresight and calculation, not production complexity or world size. Tactical variety concerns different responses to immediate situations. Comeback potential concerns recovery during ongoing play, not reloading a failed mission. Replay value concerns repeated interest, not just the amount of one-time content. Ease concerns learning the controls and rules. Speed concerns reaching a complete, satisfying play cycle; the lack of identical round/mission units makes this the least directly comparable axis across genres. The GTA VI values need revision after actual play.

This cross-genre overlay omits narrative, audiovisual craft, exploration and production value. It cannot rank overall game quality. Do not aggregate polygon areas or average the six ratings into an overall score.
