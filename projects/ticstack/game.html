Rules

Stack on any space, up to four discs high. Only the top color counts. Four in a row, column, or main diagonal wins. You cannot cover the disc your opponent just placed; let it rest for one turn. If it is the only cell with room, you may use it.

The left player starts. No passing or moving discs. All 64 spaces filled without a line: draw.

Arrow keys to choose · Enter or Space to place. Computer plays the right-hand color using bounded search, including the resting-disc rule. Perfect play is unknown.
