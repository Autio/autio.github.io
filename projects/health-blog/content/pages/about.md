# About

This is a personal blog about health, illness, and recovery. It contains reflections on navigating cancer diagnosis and treatment, written from personal experience.

## Purpose

These writings are meant to:
- Share personal experiences with health challenges
- Provide comfort to others facing similar situations
- Document the journey of recovery and healing
- Explore the intersection of illness and meaning

## Disclaimer

This blog contains personal reflections and should not be considered medical advice. Always consult with qualified healthcare professionals for medical decisions and treatment options.

## Contact

For questions or to share your own experiences, please reach out through the main site.
