# Notes on weathering cancer

This is a personal account of navigating cancer diagnosis and treatment. These notes are written from my own experience and are not medical advice.

## The diagnosis

Receiving a cancer diagnosis is like being handed a map to a country you never wanted to visit. The landscape is unfamiliar, the language foreign, and the customs strange. Yet here you are, passport stamped, luggage packed with uncertainty.

## The journey begins

The initial shock gives way to a strange kind of clarity. Suddenly, priorities rearrange themselves with startling speed. What seemed important yesterday feels trivial today. What was background noise becomes silence worth preserving.

## Treatment and its realities

Treatment brings its own rhythm - appointments, medications, side effects, and the constant negotiation between hope and realism. Each day becomes a small victory, each week a milestone.

## Finding meaning

In the midst of uncertainty, there's an opportunity to find meaning in the smallest moments. A good cup of coffee, a phone call from a friend, the way light falls through a window - these become anchors in the storm.

## Moving forward

Recovery, when it comes, is not a return to who you were before. It's an evolution into someone who carries the experience forward, integrating it into a new understanding of what it means to be alive.
