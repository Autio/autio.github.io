# Notes on weathering cancer

## Introduction

Diagnosed with stage III <span data-wikipedia="colorectal cancer">colorectal cancer</span> at 36, but cancer free with no evidence of disease at 40 thanks primarily to [Immunocine](https://immunocine.com) immune therapy and [Keytruda/Pembrolizumab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pembrolizumab) checkpoint inhibitor medication.

Benefited greatly from guidance given by [Heal Navigator](https://www.healnavigator.com), and a self-sought [companion diagnostic](https://www.foundationmedicine.com/test/foundationone-cdx#).

## Timeline

- Diagnosis: October 2021
- Radiation therapy: October 2021
- Chemo: 16 rounds total starting December 2021
- Surgical resection: April 2022
- Recurrence 2023
- Heal Navigator contacted March 2023
- Immunocine recommendation from Heal Navigator
- Immunocine March-April 2024
- Very strong initial immune reaction led to intestinal blockage, sepsis
- Keytruda: June 2024-July 2025
- Elimination of tumour: April 2024 - August 2025
- One year of NED (no evidence of disease) at August 2025

## Summary action points

Personal suggestions on what actions to take if diagnosed with cancer. Not medical advice.

- Incorporate weekly <span data-wikipedia="fasting">fasting</span> into your routine, or a fasting-mimicking diet.
- Try 24-hour water fasting. Practice water fasting for 24 hours before and after a chemo drip. This has both major protective benefits and can boost the efficacy of the medication.
- Work to maintain a plant-heavy <span data-wikipedia="ketogenic diet">ketogenic diet</span> to put cancer cells under metabolic stress.
- Create a supplement protocol with a view towards validated, peer-reviewed effects especially to protect your immune system function.
- Advocate for yourself to ensure the latest science is available for your specific diagnosis. If feasible, ask for an extended genetic panel of your tumour sample. Look for immunotherapeutic options, and latest trials. Your oncologist needs to worry about many different types of cancer. You only need to worry about one very specific diagnosis.
- If possible, find someone who can do advocacy on your behalf if dealing with the cancer makes it difficult to do so yourself.
- Explore whether there are effective repurposed medications that can help weaken or eliminate cancer cells with minimal or manageable side effects. For example: <span data-wikipedia="artemisinin">Artemisinin</span>, <span data-wikipedia="low-dose naltrexone">LDN</span>, <span data-wikipedia="fenbendazole">Fenbendazole</span>
- Maintain a social life and seek out the methods that work for you to keep a hopeful mindset.
