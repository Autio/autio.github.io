01 / Platform product leadership

mWater: evolving a global platform for water and sanitation

Since 2018, I have led product development at mWater, spanning data collection, analysis, asset management, workflows and integrations. The platform serves field staff, utilities and national governments, often with limited connectivity.

mWater describes itself as the most widely used data platform in the WASH sector. By November 2026 it had more than 400,000 registered users across 200 countries and territories. The platform recorded 52 million survey responses by September 2026.

Solstice: from an idea to adoption across sectors

When I joined mWater in 2018, Solstice was an idea for extending the platform beyond water and sanitation. I led its positioning, app and portal launch, and subsequent development with staff and partners.

By September 2026, Solstice had more than 42,000 registered users across 155 countries, with adoption extending into health and other sectors.

Independent AI safety engineering · Experimental language

Hegelese: checking semantic drift in AI-generated code

Hegelese is an experimental functional language and finite-process checker exploring semantic drift in AI-assisted programming. It checks whether a proposed rewrite preserves explicitly stated behaviours and identifies what is lost.

The checker can return a counterexample when a preservation claim fails. It offers a narrow, executable way to test for semantic drift, not a general guarantee of safe AI-generated code.

The underlying question

Can an AI change a program repeatedly without silently discarding behaviour that earlier versions were meant to preserve?
