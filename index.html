01 / Platform product leadership

mWater: evolving a global platform for water and sanitation

Since 2018, I have led product development at mWater, spanning data collection, analysis, asset management, workflows and integrations. The platform serves field staff, utilities and national governments, often with limited connectivity.

mWater describes itself as the most widely used data platform in the WASH sector. By November 2026 it had more than 400,000 registered users across 200 countries and territories. The platform recorded 52 million survey responses by September 2026.

02 / Product launch and growth

Solstice: product development beyond WASH

When I joined mWater in 2018, Solstice was an idea for extending the platform beyond water and sanitation. I led its positioning, app and portal launch, and subsequent development with staff and partners.

By September 2026, Solstice had more than 42,000 registered users across 155 countries, with adoption extending into health and other sectors.

03 / AI access

Leading AI adoption in WASH

I have taken a leading role in bringing AI into the WASH sector, from publishing mWater’s AI strategy to shaping how practitioners can use AI with their own data.

My contribution
Set out the product strategy for agent access, supported the MCP server and AI features, and helped define the safeguards for real operational data.
In practice
The MCP server gives assistants permission-aware access. Proposed data changes remain subject to human review, and the Integrator lets users connect other systems using plain language.

Venture leadership

Stellar Data

I am Venture Lead at Stellar Data, mWater Foundation’s for-profit subsidiary. We provide technical assistance, data integration, AI-powered analytics, and dedicated support for organisations using mWater and Solstice.

The work ranges from configuring apps and building data portals to helping teams develop workflows, indicators, and dashboards. All profits return to the Foundation to sustain the free mWater platform.

About me

How I came to product work

My career began in the private sector at quantitative hedge fund GSA Capital, working on trade reconciliations, treasury automation and risk management. I then developed mapping tools at the British Red Cross and worked on global information systems at WaterAid. Across those roles, I learned to value software that works reliably in practice, with the people and resources available.

I have done extensive consulting and training through UpBeam Oy, including engagements with WaterAid, the Netherlands Red Cross, and Water For People. My other interests include philosophy, writing and independent software projects.

I bring a deep personal interest in AI to hands-on research and development, especially UpBeam Science and my independent AI work →.
