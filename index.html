Data, AI & institutional capacity

Operationalising data for global governance

My work concerns how institutions put data to use: how it informs decisions, directs resources, and becomes part of the daily work of delivering public services.

Since 2018, I have led product development at mWater and Solstice, working with governments, utilities, and development partners on systems that connect field information to operational responsibilities.

I am interested in how AI can extend this capacity, and what it takes for institutions to understand, govern, and sustain the systems they rely on.

Selected work

Decisions behind the products

Two examples from my product leadership at mWater, built with our team and partners.

01 / AI access

A useful way in for AI agents

Users were bringing AI assistants to a platform built for people. They needed capable access without losing control of their data.

My role
Product leadership and the platform’s published AI strategy.
Design choice
Give agents a supported, permission-aware route in, with people reviewing proposed data changes.
In practice
The MCP server connects assistants to users’ data; the Integrator helps practitioners connect external systems in plain language.
Read the strategy and lessons →
02 / Operations

From a reported fault to a repair

Collecting a problem report is only the beginning. Someone needs to assign the work, follow it through, and check the result.

My role
Product leadership for Workflows, developed with investment from UNICEF Madagascar.
Design choice
Let organizations define their own steps, responsibilities, and reviews within the platform.
In practice
By August 2026, users had created nearly 10,000 workflow instances across about 170 types.
Read nine months of Workflows →

Much of this work takes shape with public institutions, including national systems in Zimbabwe, Uganda, Madagascar, and Papua New Guinea.

About me

Technology, institutions, and the people using both.

Before mWater, I worked on global information systems at WaterAid and mapping tools at the British Red Cross. That experience still shapes how I think about product development: the software has to fit the responsibilities, resources, and working lives around it.

I also lead Stellar’s implementation and advisory work. I have done extensive consulting and training through UpBeam Oy, including engagements with WaterAid, the Netherlands Red Cross, and Water For People. Away from the product roadmap, I explore philosophy, writing, and small interactive projects.
