Where mWater fits

No comparison table can capture every edition, add-on, or implementation. This one asks a narrower question: what does a WASH programme need to move from field observation to governed infrastructure data, decisions, service delivery, and finance?

mWater is deliberately broad, not universally deepest. Power BI is a more specialized business-intelligence product; Salesforce is a more extensive general CRM; QuickBooks is a fuller accounting package; and ArcGIS offers a much larger GIS ecosystem. mWater’s advantage is different: WASH teams can collect offline data, connect and transform external data with Integrator, maintain infrastructure and customer records, analyse and map results, coordinate work, track indicators, and manage billing in one purpose-built environment.
