# Inline illustrations for essays

Use these defaults for a diagram or interactive embedded in an essay. The D3 whirlpool in [Against Platonic Space](against-platonic-space.html#vortex-title) is the reference.

- Place the illustration beside the passage that gives it meaning. Align it with the prose and use the surrounding page background, a single thin neutral border, and generous space around the drawing.
- Draw with fine lines, muted colors, and a sparing accent. Make contrast and line style carry the distinctions that matter.
- Let the surrounding prose introduce the illustration. Keep visible text to the labels needed to operate or interpret it. In the whirlpool, that means only the three slider names.
- Give each control one clear effect. Start with settings that make the interesting relationship visible, and make reset restore those settings. Use simple, accessible icons for secondary actions like pause and reset.
- Use native keyboard-operable inputs and accessible names for icon buttons. Give the graphic a short accessible description. Honor reduced-motion preferences and keep the illustration usable while paused.
- Check the initial view, the effect of each control, and the reading flow on a narrow screen.
